# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be civil. Argue with the work, not the person. Assume the other party is acting in good
faith until they clearly are not.

## Expected behaviour

- Disagree with reasoning. "This is wrong because X" is useful; "this is wrong" is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline your change. An open source project is not obliged
  to accept every contribution, and a decline is not a judgement of you.
- Keep discussion on the technical merits in issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language of any kind
- Publishing others' private information
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Enforcement

Report problems to **conduct@nodemaven.com**. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block
accounts. We will say publicly that an action was taken and why, without identifying the
reporter.
